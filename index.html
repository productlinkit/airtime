      Is Airtime available worldwide?

      Airtime currently supports multiple countries across Asia, with more regions and features being added.

      Can I purchase without an account?

      Yes, guest checkout is fully supported. You only need to enter your email address to receive the voucher or receipt.

      What happens if a transaction fails?

      If your payment is successful but delivery fails, our automated system will flag the transaction. Contact support and we will resolve or refund it.

      Are there any extra fees?

      No hidden fees. The total payment shown before you pay already includes all admin fees, so what you see is what you pay.

      How long until the credit or data arrives?

      Most top-ups arrive within a few minutes after payment is confirmed. If it hasn't arrived after 30 minutes, contact us on WhatsApp with your receipt.

      How do refunds work?

      If the top-up can't be delivered, you get a full refund to your original payment method. Message us on WhatsApp with your receipt number and we'll process it.
